# Editorial Platform Targets

These are practical writing defaults for the research prototype, not asserted platform limits. Follow explicit user requirements when they differ.

## Xiaohongshu

- Cover ratio: 3:4.
- Title target: a specific benefit, tension, or question that remains readable on a phone.
- Body: short paragraphs; lead with the reader's problem, give the useful idea or method, then state a concrete next action.
- Hashtags: use a small, relevant set. Prefer topic and audience intent over generic traffic tags.
- Visual: use a no-text artwork plus deterministic title/subtitle overlay when wording must be exact.
- Delivery: final cover, post body, CTA, hashtags, alt text, prompt, and manifest.

## WeChat public account

- Cover ratio target: 2.35:1.
- Copy: article title, short abstract, and share text are separate assets.
- Visual: keep important subjects away from crop-sensitive edges.

## X / Twitter

- Cover ratio target: 5:2 for a wide editorial visual.
- Copy: concise post text and alt text are first-class outputs.
- Visual: keep the core message recognizable without relying on embedded body text.
